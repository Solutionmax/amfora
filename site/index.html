BEFORE YOU START

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is Amfora a hosted service?

Amfora runs on your own infrastructure, with bundled storage or an S3 compatible provider. You choose where the application and files live.

How do I get Amfora?

Amfora is publicly available on GitHub. Browse the source, download a release, or follow the installation guide. No GitHub account is required. Contact SolutionMAX for help with your installation.

Do other people need an account?

No account is required to use a shared download link or upload request. Your configured passwords, limits and expiry rules still apply.

Are the files end to end encrypted?

No. Use HTTPS for traffic and appropriate security controls on your host or storage provider. Amfora does not currently provide end to end encryption.

Do I need every sign in provider?

No. Local accounts are enough to get started. Two factor authentication is available, and external sign in providers are optional. Enable only what your team uses.
