Before you start

Straight
answers.

I run Palmr. Can I move?

Yes. Palmr was archived in February 2026; Amfora reads its data volume as it is, so users, files and links you already sent keep working. Five steps to move.

Is Amfora a hosted service?

No. It runs on your own server or VPS, with included storage or a bucket you own.

Do the people I send to need an account?

Never. A download link opens in the browser; a receive link lets them upload without signing up.

Is it really free?

Yes, all of it, under Apache-2.0. The free version shows “Powered by Amfora” on public pages; the brandpack removes that for €79 once.

How do updates work?

The app checks a signed release and can apply it in one click when the host updater is installed. Otherwise you pull the image yourself.
